# Assumptions and unresolved evidence

This file distinguishes documented facts from choices made to produce a usable scroll-controlled local prototype. Sources are linked in `sources.md`; extracted CAD limitations are in `asset-audit.md`; completion work is in `../ASSET_GAPS.md`.

| ID | Assumption or conflict | Evidence | Implementation consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A01 | Standard two-group AV, normal low supports; no serial/year supplied. | User request; official AV front image. | Exclude MP paddles, ABR scales and PB X independent boilers. Use AV three-button groups. Do not claim a serial-specific bill of materials. |
| A02 | Overall nominal height is 533mm. | Product table/brochure 53.3cm; parts catalog drawing says 530mm. Actual tessellated CAD is710×642.3×537.3mm with projections/supports included. | Retain actual CAD geometry/scale and use710×590×533mm as the nominal comparator. No undocumented source rescaling; actual bounds remain in the asset audit. |
| A03 | The reference photograph has the later `F.3.040.01` shower screen. | V3.2 p36 splits at serial0354; V3.8 p49 retains this screen. | Model later shallow H6.5 screen, not both screen versions at once. Actual serial applicability remains unverified. |
| A04 | Silicone `H.3.007` gasket selected for explicit dimensions. | Catalog lists both silicone and NBR options, without target serial choice. | Annulus 71.6/55×8.2mm is supported. Bevel, compression and color are approximate; do not claim OEM-identical elastomer profile. |
| A05 | Standard common coffee boiler chosen as `L.1.005.2GB.R`. | V3.2 p47 and V3.8 p61 distinguish standard/right-side 2GR and3GR spare bodies. | Keep two saturated necks on a common transverse boiler. The alternative right-side spare is not silently substituted. |
| A06 | Pump module is external. Its visualization position is editorial. | Operating manual V3.2 p11; catalog p78. | Never install it within chassis. No claim is made that a side-of-scene location is the required installation position. |
| A07 | Boiler cylinder dimensions and exact port centers are inferred unless present in manufacturer CAD. | Capacity, insulation size and perspective exploded diagrams; no vessel shop drawing. | Do not calculate an “exact” vessel solely from liters. Tanks retain visual-approximation status if reconstructed. |
| A08 | Frame cross sections, metal thickness and mounting centers are inferred when not provided by CAD. | Catalog p6 shows structure but no frame part dimensions or part number. | Fabricated frame elements cannot be classified as verified CAD or dimensionally complete. |

| A11 | White/black satin body materials are user-requested custom finish. | User brief; product brochure permits custom RAL colors. | Finish is artistic. No claim of a particular approved RAL code or paint specification. |
| A12 | Locking angle for portafilters and bolt-turn choreography is illustrative. | Manual p14 gives left-to-right locking direction without angle. | Small sensible rotation; no360° flourish or specified factory torque. |

| A14 | Public manufacturer downloads have no explicit permissive reuse license. | No license grant found within resources inspected. | Keep provenance; do not relabel OEM assets as project-owned. Public deployment of converted CAD needs rights review. |
| A15 | Authoring a mesh from an exploded diagram confirms topology, not metrology. | No dimensional manufacturing drawings for most internal components. | Accuracy states remain separate: Verified CAD / Document-supported reconstruction / Visual approximation / Missing asset. Mixed assemblies disclose their weakest material dimension/placement uncertainty. |

The `Document-supported reconstruction` classification means the part identity and relevant relationships are supported by an official source. It does not certify clearances, machining tolerances, fluid pathways or loaded physical behavior. A `Verified CAD` exterior designer node may be authentic geometry yet still be an inadequate representation of a hidden internal component.
