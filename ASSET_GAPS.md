# Asset gaps and completion requirements

The project can demonstrate deterministic assembly, camera direction, **142 independent extracted manufacturer CAD nodes**, the genuine localized hot-water outlet subassembly, and **12 independently controlled lower AV group reconstructions**. It cannot certify a complete engineering-accurate internal Linea PB from exterior designer resources and perspective diagrams alone. Runtime reconstructions remain explicitly classified in their part manifest and source UI.

The CAD-specific inventory and independent-object availability are in `docs/asset-audit.md`. The source has no steam/coffee boiler, external pump, complete hydraulics or full saturated group bodies. The implemented group skirt is only the exposed lower outline; no full neck/cap/flowmeter internals have been invented. Sources below refer to `docs/sources.md`. The existence of a source part in a catalog is not the availability of its separate mesh.

To promote any part to `Verified CAD`, retain the original resource URL/hash, original node identity, units and conversion transform, and verify its final machine coordinates. To promote a reconstruction beyond a visual approximation, collect the missing measurements above and document the reconstruction constraints. The complete final acceptance criteria remain blocked for parts whose physical geometry/placement are still approximate; a visually credible scene must not conceal that distinction.
